Owly charges platform trading fees through Hyperliquid's builder fee mechanism. Strategies use different builder fee rates, with base rates ranging from **2–10 bps (0.02%–0.1%)**, depending on the selected strategy's fee configuration. Eligible referral discounts are then applied to the applicable base rate.

## Platform Trading Fees

| Product | Fee | When Applied |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Copy Trading** | **2–10 bps (0.02%–0.1%)**, according to the specific copy-trading strategy's builder fee configuration | On executed trading volume |
| **Trade terminal** | **0.02% (2 bps)** | On executed terminal trading volume |
| **Grid and other configured bots** | **2–10 bps (0.02%–0.1%)**, according to the specific strategy's builder fee configuration | On executed bot trading volume |

One basis point (bps) is **0.01%**. For example, suppose a strategy has a base builder fee of **5.5 bps (0.055%)**. A 10,000 USDC trade incurs a platform fee before discounts of `10,000 × 0.055% = 5.50 USDC`. The 5.5 bps rate is illustrative; actual strategy base rates range from 2–10 bps, depending on the strategy configuration.

<Note>
Platform trading fees vary by strategy configuration. The terminal's 2 bps rate does not establish the rate for every strategy. Check the strategy's applicable builder fee and discounts before starting or resuming it.
</Note>

## Builder Authorization Is a Ceiling

The standard builder authorization requests a maximum rate of **0.1% (10 bps)**. This is permission for a maximum charge, not an upfront payment or the rate charged on every trade. The actual builder fee submitted with an order determines its platform trading fee, within the approved limit.

Some account onboarding flows may check an existing approval against their own required minimum. An approval value alone does not establish the fee a strategy will charge.

## Referral Discounts

During the applicable discount window, eligible referees receive a **5% reduction in the applicable builder fee** for supported Owly-managed strategies, regardless of the inviter's tier. The duration follows the configuration applicable at binding. This fee reduction is not a separate withdrawable rebate. See [Referral System](/guide/referral).

Referral rewards are calculated from actual eligible builder fees after any discount, using each recipient's applicable L1 or L2 ratio. They are not calculated from the full trading volume or Hyperliquid's exchange fees. The discount window starts at referral binding; its expiry alone does not end referral rewards.

## Performance and Management Fees

The current trading flow does not add a platform performance fee or management fee to the builder trading charge. Profitability still depends on trading fees, funding, execution prices, and other costs.

## Other Costs

The platform trading fee is separate from:

- **Hyperliquid exchange fees**, including maker/taker fees and any applicable exchange fee discounts.
- **Perpetual funding payments**, which can be paid or received while holding positions.
- **Network, bridge, swap, or withdrawal charges** associated with moving funds or using external services.
- **Borrowing costs** when using an On-chain Lending account.

Review the transaction or quote shown for a funding or withdrawal operation. A zero platform performance fee does not mean that trading or moving funds is free.

<Tip>
Compare returns after fees and funding. The fee approved in your wallet, the platform builder fee on a fill, and Hyperliquid's exchange fee are different values.
</Tip>
