## Overview

**Owly** brings trader discovery, copy trading, a trading terminal, and automated strategies to [Hyperliquid](https://hyperliquid.xyz). Use account management to connect your trading wallet or a supported protocol account; the website Assistant can explain the available workflows.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="users" href="/introduction/who-is-owly-for#followers" title="For Followers">
    Explore trader histories and configure automated copying with your chosen account and risk settings.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="crown" href="/introduction/who-is-owly-for#curators" title="For Curators">
    Manage strategy accounts and delegate view, start, or stop permissions to registered fund managers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Core Mission

We believe that access to sophisticated trading strategies should not be limited to professional traders. Owly democratizes trading by:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover">
    Browse and analyze performance data of verified traders on the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Add an API Wallet account, native Hyperliquid sub-account, or LeverAcc account and complete its required authorizations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor">
    Track bot status, fills, positions, and returns; review risk settings and stop strategies when needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Features

| Feature | Current behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Trader discovery** | Explore traders using performance and risk metrics |
| **Copy trading** | Account-based strategy settings, market filters, and portfolio risk thresholds |
| **Trade terminal** | Manual trading, integrated funding, and read-only Ghost observation |
| **Grid Bot** | Perpetual neutral, long, and short grids from the dedicated Trading Bots page |
| **Accounts** | Generated API Wallet authorization, native sub-account setup, and LeverAcc (Alpha) |
| **Portfolio** | Account value, PnL, cash-flow-aware returns, and operation history |
