## 2026.11.08

### New

- **Expanded trading controls**: Added Scale orders, mobile TWAP orders, and position take-profit and stop-loss controls.
- **Guided trader selection**: Filter traders by available capital, strategy, and historical drawdown, with strategy-tag search and a more compact Discovery toolbar.
- **Favorites and blacklist management**: Added separate tabs, wallet-scoped blacklist storage and confirmed import, plus background synchronization of preference changes.
- **Customizable trading workspace**: Resize and save the chart and order-book layout, and view fill execution prices directly on candles.

### Improvements

- **Strategy-aware copy settings**: Adapt default copy settings to the source trader’s strategy and recent exchange activity, with clearer authorization and setup guidance.
- **Faster account and trader views**: Improved live data updates, paginated trade history and favorites, and loaded key rows before performance charts.
- **Portfolio responsiveness**: Reduced initial loading and account-switching layout shifts, and kept navigation and account actions responsive during background refreshes.
- **Mobile trading**: Refined account details, order history, trading dialogs, market selection, and connection-status explanations across devices.

### Fixes

- **Returns and asset totals**: Unified cash-flow-adjusted return handling across account views and corrected missing or inconsistent portfolio-margin, BLP, outcome, and spot asset values.
- **Trading continuity**: Improved market reconnection, preserved account tabs when switching markets, and kept account actions and cancellation authorization tied to the correct account.
- **Wallet and history feedback**: Improved restored-session handling, displayed login times in the local timezone, and showed actual referral-reward settlement times.
