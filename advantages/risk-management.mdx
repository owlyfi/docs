Owly provides controls for selecting what to copy, synchronizing positions, and responding to configured loss or profit thresholds. Their effect depends on the account and strategy configuration.

## Risk Control Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stop Loss and Take Profit" icon="hand">
    Configure strategy loss, take-profit, and drawdown-from-high thresholds. Leaving a threshold blank does not establish a protective percentage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Copy Scope" icon="filter">
    Select perpetual or spot copying, included exchanges, and coin inclusion or exclusion rules.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Position Catch-up Filter" icon="gauge">
    An ROE threshold can exclude source positions from catch-up synchronization to reduce chasing positions that have already moved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Liquidity Filter" icon="sliders">
    Optionally skip perpetual markets below the system's minimum open interest or daily trading volume requirements.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Current Copy Settings

These are the starting values of the standard advanced-settings form. Existing accounts retain their saved configuration, and a guided setup can change the proposed settings before confirmation.

| Setting | Starting Value | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Copy multiplier | **1×** | Scales the copy ratio; it is not a guarantee of equal position sizes or returns |
| Stop loss / take profit / drawdown stop | **Not set** | Enter the protective thresholds you want to use |
| ROE catch-up filter | **Enabled, 10%** | Filters source positions for catch-up synchronization; it does not stop copying subsequent new orders or fills |
| PE Ratio Cap | **30%** | Caps effective copy capital relative to source account value; this is not a maximum percentage per position |
| Leverage synchronization | **Enabled, 83 seconds** | Follows the source trader's leverage settings; this is not a fixed maximum leverage cap |
| Position synchronization | **61 seconds** | Periodic synchronization when perpetual copying is enabled |
| Skip low-liquidity markets | **Off** | When enabled, applies system-owned market eligibility thresholds |

## Portfolio SL/TP

The [portfolio overview](/guide/dashboard) offers a separate strategy covering a group of copy trading accounts. It has loss, take-profit, and drawdown-from-high thresholds, along with tracked baselines and a configurable check interval. Review the covered accounts and trigger values when saving or recalibrating it.

A portfolio trigger is different from a stop order attached to one position. The portfolio control and the individual account's thresholds should be reviewed together.

## How to Use the Controls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the Account and Source">
    Confirm the funded trading account, source trader, copy multiplier, markets, and any setup risk messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Explicit Thresholds">
    Enter the stop-loss and take-profit values you need, then review the confirmation. Empty fields are not default protection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor the Saved Strategy">
    Check strategy status, positions, account value, and configured check intervals. A position or order filter can also mean that some source activity is not copied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Protective Actions">
    After a trigger or manual stop, inspect the account's actual status and remaining positions. Stopping copying and closing positions are separate actions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Risk controls depend on data and execution. Market gaps, liquidity, outages, and check intervals can cause exits beyond a threshold. They do not guarantee principal protection or a specific maximum loss.
</Note>

<Tip>
Begin with a smaller allocation, inspect the difference between your fills and the source trader's fills, and adjust only after understanding the account's behavior.
</Tip>
