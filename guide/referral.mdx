# Owlyfi Referral System

Share your referral code or link to invite users to Owly. When a friend's eligible trading accounts generate builder fees included in settlement, you can earn direct (L1) rewards. Users invited by that friend can also generate indirect (L2) rewards for you. Invited users can receive a temporary platform trading fee discount under the applicable rules.

## Two Referral Levels

Suppose A invites B, and B invites C:

| User generating fees | Reward recipient | Applicable ratio |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| B | A earns L1 rewards | A's tier L1 ratio |
| C | B earns L1 rewards | B's tier L1 ratio |
| C | A earns L2 rewards | A's tier L2 ratio |

The system settles only L1 and L2 rewards. Each recipient uses their own applicable tier ratio. C's fee discount follows the tier rules of their direct inviter, B.

## Referral Tiers & Rewards

The referral system supports Normal, Advanced, and VIP tiers. The default rules are shown below; platform configuration can change the effective values:

| Tier | Cumulative direct-referral fee threshold | L1 reward | L2 reward | Referee fee reduction | Discount duration | Direct invite limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Normal | $0 | 30% | 5% | 5% | 14 days | 5 users |
| Advanced | $10 | 35% | 10% | 10% | 14 days | 20 users |
| VIP | $500 | 40% | 15% | 15% | 14 days | Unlimited |

<Note>
These values come from the initial tier configuration and do not establish the current policy in every environment. Check your current tier, invite allowance, available rewards, and reward records on the **Referral** page.
</Note>

Automatic tier evaluation uses **cumulative builder fees in reward records from active direct referrals (L1)**. L2 fees, trading volume, and invite counts do not determine the tier. Tiers are re-evaluated after settlement, and later settlements use the updated applicable ratios; previously settled rewards are not automatically recalculated after a tier change. Some accounts may have an assigned tier or a separately adjusted invite allowance.

A direct invitation uses an invite slot when the relationship is bound, even if the user has not traded. Indirect invitations do not use your direct invite slots.

## Reward Calculation Base

Platform builder fees vary by strategy, with base rates ranging from **2–10 bps (0.02%–0.1%)**, according to the selected strategy's builder fee configuration. The same trading volume can generate different fees and referral rewards across strategies. Eligible referral discounts also affect the actual fee paid.

Rewards equal **actual builder fees in eligible settled trading windows × the recipient's applicable reward ratio**. The fee base comes from the invited user's currently active trading sub-accounts included in settlement. Do not assume that all trading by their wallet on other accounts or platforms qualifies.

Lower fees paid by an invited user also reduce the reward base. Hyperliquid exchange fees, funding payments, and network costs are excluded from this builder fee base.

## How Referee Discounts Work

When a referral is bound, the system sets a discount expiry using the direct inviter's applicable tier rules. The discount starts **when the relationship is bound**, without waiting for the first trade. The default duration is 14 days; the configuration applicable at binding determines the actual duration. This is separate from the browser's referral attribution window.

- During the discount window, eligible Owly-managed strategies can apply a reduced builder fee automatically.
- The discount ratio follows the inviter's applicable tier configuration; it is not necessarily frozen at registration.
- An inviter's tier upgrade does not automatically extend an existing discount expiry. Discount expiry alone does not end the referral relationship or referral rewards.
- Discounts reduce the trading fee charged. They do not create a separate cash rebate that the invited user must withdraw.
- Strategies using a third-party builder or a preserved independent low fee are not overwritten by the standard discount rules. Check the applicable fee before trading.

Discounts use the selected strategy's applicable base fee. For example, suppose a strategy has a base builder fee of **5.5 bps (0.055%)** and the referee receives a 5% fee reduction. The theoretical discounted rate is `5.5 × (1 − 5%) = 5.225 bps`. The 5.5 bps rate is illustrative; actual strategy base rates range from 2–10 bps, depending on the strategy configuration. The submitted order rate is rounded up to the trading interface's supported precision. Rewards always use actual fees recorded on fills.

See [Fee Structure](/faq/fees) for the difference between trading fees and the builder authorization ceiling.

## Referral Attribution

Owly uses **first-touch attribution within the same browser** before registration:

1. A referral link with a correctly formatted code stores the first code in a browser cookie for up to **30 days**. The code's existence and remaining capacity are checked at registration or binding.
2. While that cookie remains valid, later referral links do not replace it. Referral capture does not replace an existing signed-in user's inviter.
3. Registration attempts to use the stored code. The backend validates the code and remaining invite capacity, rejecting requests carrying invalid or exhausted codes. If automatic registration with a code fails, the current page shows an error and retries without the code. Successful registration does not confirm referral binding; check your inviter on the Referral page.

This cookie is not a permanent account binding: clearing cookies, expiry, or using another browser or device can remove the stored attribution. Once the account has an inviter, the normal binding flow does not allow replacing that inviter. If no inviter was applied at registration, an eligible user can enter a code once on the Referral page.

Referral codes contain **4–16 letters, numbers, underscores, or hyphens** and are matched without case sensitivity. Binding is subject to code validity, available invite capacity, and referral eligibility checks.

## Earnings & Settlement

A scheduled task fetches eligible trading fees and calculates rewards for each trading sub-account's unsettled window. On successful settlement, reward records and the available reward balance are updated together. Retrying a task does not pay the same successfully settled window twice.

Settlement allows a delay for data synchronization. Task scheduling, account settlement status, and upstream data availability affect when records appear. A trade does not immediately create a withdrawable reward; a temporary API failure requires a later successful settlement.

An invitation can remain `pending` until eligible fee-generating activity has been recorded. The Referral page distinguishes invited users, users who have traded, reward records, and withdrawal history.

### Historical Trades and Settlement Time

- A new trading account's first settlement establishes a starting point without automatically rewarding all earlier historical trades. Binding a code later also does not guarantee retroactive rewards.
- Historical accounts awaiting reconciliation must complete it before settlement can continue. Historical rewards are not automatically backfilled.
- A reward record's **settlement time** is when the reward was actually settled and credited. Its settlement date is the task's accounting date; it does not establish that every underlying fee occurred on that date. Record creation time is also distinct from settlement time. Historical records may have no settlement time; follow the page display.

### Withdrawals

1. Open **Referral** and check your withdrawable reward balance.
2. Enter an amount within that balance. The current withdrawal form requires at least **1 USDC**; the applicable withdrawal rules may impose a higher minimum.
3. Submit the request. The requested amount is deducted from your available reward balance while it is processed.
4. Follow the request in withdrawal history and check its final status and transaction reference.

| Status | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| `pending` | Submitted for review |
| `approved` | Approved for processing |
| `processing` | Processing is underway |
| `completed` | Recorded as completed with a transaction reference |
| `rejected` / `failed` | Review rejected the request, or processing failed; the amount is returned to the available reward balance, with the reason in the record |

<Note>
Submission is not an immediate on-chain transfer. Use the withdrawal record to confirm the outcome; this guide does not guarantee a fixed processing time.
</Note>

## Calculation Example

Suppose A and B both use the default Normal ratios, A invites B, and B invites C. This example assumes an **actual builder fee rate of 5.5 bps (0.055%)** on fills, after any applicable discounts and fee rounding:

1. B trades 10,000 USDC, generating actual builder fees of `10,000 × 0.055% = 5.50 USDC`. A earns L1 rewards of `5.50 × 30% = 1.65 USDC`.
2. C trades 20,000 USDC, generating actual builder fees of `20,000 × 0.055% = 11.00 USDC`. B earns L1 rewards of `11.00 × 30% = 3.30 USDC`, and A earns L2 rewards of `11.00 × 5% = 0.55 USDC`.
3. A earns `1.65 + 0.55 = 2.20 USDC` in total. C's same fees serve as the base for B's L1 and A's L2 rewards separately.

The 5.5 bps rate is an example assumption, not a uniform rate for every strategy. The example reward ratios do not establish the current tier policy either. Use the applicable rules and your settled reward records for actual amounts.

## Eligibility

Self-referrals and circular referral chains are rejected. Referral codes also have usage limits unless the applicable tier or account allowance permits otherwise. Do not use fabricated accounts or artificial activity to evade the referral rules.
